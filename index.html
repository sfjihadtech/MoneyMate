Privacy

Your Privacy Matters

MoneyMate is designed to help you manage your personal finances while keeping your information secure.

We Collect

  • Account information (if you sign in)
  • Transaction data
  • Budget and savings information
  • App preferences (language, currency, theme)

We Do Not Collect

  • Bank passwords
  • Credit or debit card PINs
  • Biometric data
  • Personal financial credentials

Data Storage

Your financial data is stored securely on your device. If Cloud Sync is enabled in the future, your data will be encrypted before transmission.

Premium Subscription

If you subscribe to MoneyMate Premium, purchase and billing information is processed entirely by the app store (such as Google Play Billing) and is never seen or stored by MoneyMate directly.

Your Control

You can export, import, back up, or delete your data at any time.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions
Is MoneyMate free?
Yes. The core features are available for free.
What is included in Premium?
MoneyMate gives you full access to every feature for your first 7 days — Advanced Analytics, AI Financial Insights, Cloud Backup & Sync, PDF export, PIN Lock and Biometric Authentication, Premium Themes and App Icons, Recurring Transactions, Advanced Search & Filters, and more. After the trial, an active subscription is required to keep using MoneyMate.
Do I need an account?
No. You can use Guest Mode or create an account for backup and sync features.
Is my financial data secure?
Yes. Your financial information is stored securely.
Can I export my data?
Yes. You can export your financial records whenever needed.
Can I restore my backup?
Yes. Premium members can restore their most recent Cloud Backup at any time from Settings → Restore.
Can I change the language?
Yes. You can switch between English and Bangla from Settings.
Can I use multiple currencies?
Yes. MoneyMate supports multiple display currencies.
What happens if I uninstall the app?
If you don't back up your data, it may be lost after uninstalling the app.
How do I contact support?
Go to Settings → Contact Support, and our team will get back to you as soon as possible.

Getting Started
Setting up your first account
Go to Settings → My Accounts → Add Account to link or manually create your first account, then start logging transactions from the + button.
Choosing Guest Mode vs an account
Guest Mode lets you try MoneyMate instantly with data stored only on this device. Creating an account unlocks backup and multi-device sync (Premium).
Managing Transactions
Adding an income or expense
Tap the + button on the Home or Transactions tab, choose Income or Expense, fill in the amount and category, then save.
Editing or deleting a transaction
Open any transaction from your history and use the Edit or Delete option at the bottom of the detail screen.
Searching and filtering transactions
Use the search bar on the Transactions tab, or tap the filter icon for Advanced Search & Filters (Premium).
Budgets & Goals
Creating a monthly budget
Go to Settings → Monthly Budget → New Budget, pick a category and a limit, and MoneyMate will track your spending against it automatically.
Setting a savings goal
Go to Settings → Savings Goals to create a goal with a target amount and date, then log contributions as you save.
Backup & Security
Backing up your data
Premium members can back up their data from Settings → Backup. Free users can still Export Data (CSV) as a manual backup.
Securing the app with PIN or biometrics
Premium members can enable PIN Lock and Biometric Authentication from Settings → Security.
Premium & Billing
What does Premium include?
Everything in the app: Advanced Analytics, AI Insights, Cloud Backup & Sync, PDF Export, Premium Themes & Icons, PIN/Biometric Lock, Recurring Transactions, Advanced Filters, and priority support. There are no separate feature tiers once you are subscribed.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Premium subscriptions are managed through your app store account and can be cancelled there at any time.

Contact Support

We usually respond within 24 hours. You can also email us directly at support@moneymate.app.
